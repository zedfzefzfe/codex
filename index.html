Project Story

Starting with a 90s air-cooled platform, the goal is to craft a motorcycle that feels at home on open highways and mountain switchbacks. Every upgrade has a purpose: more torque, better handling, and a cockpit designed for long days in the saddle.

Design Philosophy

Keep the silhouette timeless while integrating modern lighting, custom upholstery, and navigation-ready instrumentation.

Performance Focus

Suspension overhaul, forged wheels, and a tuned exhaust combine for confident handling on mixed terrain.

Ride Experience

Balanced ergonomics, wind protection, and audio-ready helmet integration make every ride effortless.
